description: Clearly restate request to confirm alignment before work begins
---

State what I ask for, in clear, concise language, so I can confirm that we are
aligned.

- Restate the outstanding request from the entire conversation, with all
  refinements and decisions. Exclude completed work and requirements that later
  decisions replace or cancel.
- State the request in clearer words. Do not repeat it word for word.
- Keep the goal and important requirements.
- Do not add details or start the work.
- End with “Is that correct?”
